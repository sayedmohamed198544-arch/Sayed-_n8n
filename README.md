## 🌟 Overview

A modern, high-converting, responsive single-page portfolio website designed for **Sayed Mohamed**. The site is crafted with a SaaS & enterprise technology consulting aesthetic, targeted at Business Owners, HR Directors, Operations Managers, and Recruitment Agencies.

It features complete **multilingual support (English & Arabic with instant RTL/LTR switching)**, **theme customization (Dark & Light Mode)**, and enterprise-grade micro-interactions.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🌐 Multilingual Support (English & Arabic)
- Instant, client-side language switching without page reload.
- **RTL / LTR Dynamic Adaptation**: When Arabic (`العربية`) is selected, `dir="rtl"` and `lang="ar"` are set on the `<html>` root, applying the **Cairo** modern Arabic typography, right-aligning layouts, flipping timelines, modals, badges, and icons.
- Complete, natural, professional Arabic translation across every single heading, paragraph, skill tag, case study, and form label.
- Persistent language choice saved in `localStorage`.

### 2. 🌓 Dark & Light Theme System
- **Dark Mode by default** using deep slate `#0F172A`, midnight blue `#1E40AF`, and glowing sky cyan `#38BDF8`.
- **Light Mode** using crisp slate `#F8FAFC`, clean white cards `#FFFFFF`, and rich indigo/blue contrast.
- Smooth transitions between themes with no layout shifts.
- Persistent theme selection saved in `localStorage`.

### 3. ✍️ Hero Section & Dynamic Typing Effect
- Language-aware typewriter animation cycling through specialized titles:
  - *AI Automation Architect / مهندس أتمتة العمليات بالذكاء الاصطناعي*
  - *n8n Workflow Specialist / خبير تصميم مسارات العمل على n8n*
  - *Autonomous AI Agents Developer / مطور وكلاء الذكاء الاصطناعي*
  - *Power BI & HR Analytics Expert / استشاري تحليلات الموارد البشرية وPower BI*
  - *Business Process Optimization Consultant / استشاري تطوير وتحسين العمليات التشغيلية*
- Interactive high-tech SVG workflow diagram showing live data pipelines connecting AI Agents, n8n webhook nodes, HR Operations, and Power BI charts.
- Animated counter badges (15+ Years Domain Experience, 100+ Workflows, 85% Manual Workload Reduction).

### 4. 📊 Scroll Reading Progress Bar
- A glowing top-fixed 3.5px gradient bar tracking real-time reading progress across the document.

### 5. ⚡ Floating Quick Action Controls
- **WhatsApp Direct Connect**: Floating pulsing button linking directly to WhatsApp with pre-filled consultation message.
- **LinkedIn Quick Access**: Direct link to professional profile.
- **Floating Back-to-Top**: Smooth scrolling with visibility threshold.

### 6. 📄 Executive CV Preview & Print Modal
- Interactive modal showing Sayed's complete executive summary, core capabilities, and milestones, with a dedicated **Print / Save as PDF** action.

### 7. 💼 Categorized Skills Matrix & Projects
- 5 distinct discipline cards (AI & Automation, Automation Platforms, Data & Analytics, Human Resources, Business Operations) with category filter chips.
- 5 featured projects with interactive mini UI previews and clickable "Explore Architecture" modals with problem, solution, step-by-step pipeline execution, and ROI metrics.

### 8. 🧮 Interactive HR Automation ROI Calculator
- Interactive sliders for organizational headcount and weekly manual hours, generating live estimates of hours saved, cost savings, and operational speedup.

### 9. 📬 Contact Suite & Validation
- Direct contact cards (Email & Phone with instant 1-click clipboard copy).
- Social links for LinkedIn, WhatsApp, GitHub, YouTube, and Facebook.
- Form validation with error states, draft persistence via `localStorage`, and feedback toast notification.
